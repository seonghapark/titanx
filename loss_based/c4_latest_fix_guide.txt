================================================================================
                         WHAT THIS ERROR MEANS
================================================================================

The error occurred because:

1. Your refactoring removed DCP checkpoints and forced HF-only loading
2. HF model loading uses state_dict adapter to convert weights
3. The _permute() method reshapes tensors for RoPE weights
4. These tensors are FSDP-sharded across 48 ranks
5. The reshape target dimension (16) is not divisible by 48 ranks
6. PyTorch cannot automatically reshard for this operation

The fix:
- Convert distributed tensor to local before reshape
- Reshape works on local tensor without constraints
- Result is automatically synchronized across ranks
